Cycling in NYC is one of the highest-risk daily activities in the city. Most serious injuries happen to experienced riders who let their guard down — not beginners. The checklist below is not optional.
  • Helmet is non-negotiable — a single fall at 12 mph without a helmet can be fatal. No exceptions, no short trips.
  • Dooring kills — a car door opening in your path gives you less than 0.5 seconds to react. Ride 4 feet from parked cars always.
  • Trucks turn wide and silently — never pass a truck on the right at an intersection. Wait behind it.
  • Your guanfacine lowers blood pressure — sudden hard braking or a near-miss can cause momentary dizziness. Stay calm, ride predictably.
  • Assume drivers cannot see you — make eye contact before proceeding through any intersection.
